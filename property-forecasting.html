Phase 01 · Investment Situation

The Real Estate Asset Management Context

In institutional property asset management (e.g., Frasers Property, real estate investment trusts, and property funds), multi-tenant commercial and mixed-use assets generate hundreds of thousands of dollars in monthly cash flows. Managing these assets requires rigorous forecasts of **Net Operating Income (NOI)** to support bank loan underwriting, quarterly fund revaluations, and capital expenditure (CapEx) allocations.

Phase 02 · The Problem with Spreadsheets

The Limitations of Static DCF Underwriting

Asset managers historically relied on static Excel underwriting models with critical vulnerabilities:

  • Naive Linear Escalations: Assuming constant flat +3% rent growth regardless of macroeconomic downturns or supply gluts.
  • Ignoring Seasonal Occupancy Waves: Commercial leasing experiences pronounced seasonal dip-and-rebound patterns (Q2 leasing peaks vs. Q4 holidays) that static models completely miss.
  • No Real-Time Stress Testing: When interest rates spike, asset values drop simultaneously through rising capitalization rates and tenant rent concessions. Spreadsheet templates failed to model these coupled variables.
"The investment committee needed an empirical machine learning pipeline that backtests historical momentum, projects realistic forward cash flows, and automatically stress-tests asset values."
Phase 03 · Solution Architecture

The Predictive Machine Learning Architecture

I engineered an object-oriented Python pipeline incorporating:

  1. Trigonometric Cyclical Encodings: Transforming monthly calendar dates into sin(2πm/12) and cos(2πm/12) to capture smooth annual leasing seasonality.
  2. Autoregressive Lags & Momentum: Formulating multi-step historical features (Lag_1, Lag_2, Lag_3, Lag_12) and rolling statistical windows (RollingMean3, RollingStd3).
  3. Leak-Free Walk-Forward Cross-Validation: Utilizing TimeSeriesSplit(n_splits=5) to strictly prevent future data leakage.
  4. Automated Institutional Valuation: Computing Direct Capitalization and Discounted Cash Flow valuations under shifting cap rate assumptions.

# 1. Feature Engineering: Lags, Rolling Stats & Cyclical Encodings
def create_lagged_and_rolling_features(df: pd.DataFrame, target_col: str = "Net_Operating_Income") -> pd.DataFrame:
    df_feat = df.copy()
    
    # Trigonometric Cyclical Month Encodings
    df_feat["Month_Sin"] = np.sin(2 * np.pi * df_feat["Date"].dt.month / 12)
    df_feat["Month_Cos"] = np.cos(2 * np.pi * df_feat["Date"].dt.month / 12)
    
    # Autoregressive Lags (t-1, t-2, t-3, t-12)
    df_feat[f"{target_col}_Lag1"] = df_feat[target_col].shift(1)
    df_feat[f"{target_col}_Lag2"] = df_feat[target_col].shift(2)
    df_feat[f"{target_col}_Lag3"] = df_feat[target_col].shift(3)
    df_feat[f"{target_col}_Lag12"] = df_feat[target_col].shift(12)  # YoY seasonality
    
    # Rolling Momentum Window (Preventing lookahead bias)
    df_feat[f"{target_col}_RollingMean3"] = df_feat[target_col].shift(1).rolling(3).mean()
    df_feat[f"{target_col}_RollingStd3"] = df_feat[target_col].shift(1).rolling(3).std()
    
    return df_feat.dropna().reset_index(drop=True)
