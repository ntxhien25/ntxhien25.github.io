The Situation

Contractual Complexity & Trade Credit Exposure

In large industrial engineering, structural steel, and EPC contracting operations, trade credit is an essential competitive tool. However, credit terms vary widely across contracts (30, 45, 60, 70, and 75 days) and payments arrive through complex milestone draws, bank guarantees, and partial advances.

At any given point, the enterprise manages tens of billions in outstanding receivables. Managing cash flow requires continuous alignment between physical delivery milestones, commercial contract terms, and general ledger records across all counterparties.

Before the workflow

From an emailed summary to a clearer view of receivables

The previous report summarised outstanding balances by customer and debt category, with collection follow-up communicated by email. Management needed a view that could be filtered by customer, responsible staff member and receivables status.

The solution and outcome

Customer and staff views for collection follow-up

The workflow brings invoice balances, due dates, customer terms and staff assignments into an Excel dashboard. Customer, staff and status filters let management move from an overall balance to the invoices that need attention.

  • Compare current and previous receivables balances.
  • Review invoice ageing and upcoming due dates.
  • Identify the staff members responsible for following up with each customer.
  • Check that customer and staff breakdowns reconcile to the invoice ledger.

The dashboard gives management a clearer view of receivables and helps focus collection follow-up.

Tool selection

Why Excel suited this team

The team already used Excel for day-to-day accounting and reporting. An Excel dashboard kept the workflow familiar: staff could filter the report, inspect the invoice records and share the workbook through the existing email process.

Power BI or a web application could support a different reporting setup. In this case, Excel met the team's needs without introducing another interface to learn and maintain.

Formulas, native charts and reconciliation checks keep the calculations visible alongside the management view.

Reconciliation controls

Checks behind the dashboard

Source and amount checks

  • Only records with an outstanding receivables balance greater than zero are included for review.
  • Invoice gross amount less receipts applied must equal the outstanding balance.
  • Current and prior invoice schedules support the period comparison.

Coverage checks

  • Customer totals reconcile to the selected invoice balance.
  • Staff totals include every selected invoice.
  • Ageing bands reconcile to the selected invoice balance.
View the invoice amount check
=SUM('Current AR'!G6:G160)
 - SUM('Current AR'!H6:H160)
 - SUM('Current AR'!I6:I160)

A zero result confirms that gross amounts less receipts equal the outstanding balance.
